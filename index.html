Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 4, 2025

NumLoop ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile game NumLoop.

1. Information We Collect

  • Nickname: If you choose to set a nickname for the leaderboard, it is stored on our server (Supabase).
  • Game scores: Your best scores are stored to display on the leaderboard.
  • Anonymous identifier: A random ID is generated to associate your scores without requiring personal login.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily sign in with Google or Apple.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not serve ads and do not share data with advertising networks.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Used to store leaderboard scores and nicknames.
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In: Optional login for leaderboard identity.
  • Google Play Games / Apple Game Center: Used for achievements and leaderboards.

4. Data Retention

Leaderboard data (nickname and score) is retained as long as the service is active. You may request deletion by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

NumLoop does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Account & Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and all associated data (nickname, scores), please send an email to:
798@mets.co.kr
with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 days.
